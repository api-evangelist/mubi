Éric Rohmer in Out 1: Noli Me Tangere (Jacques Rivette, 1971). In 1946, the prestigious French publishing house Editions Gallimard published a novel called Élisabeth by a man named Gilbert Cordier. No one had heard of this Cordier before, or knew anything about him, for the simple reason that he didn’t exist.
